# UV Predict Support

Need help with UV Predict? We are here to help.

UV Predict helps you understand local UV conditions, plan outdoor time, and keep your recorded Time in Daylight in context. Forecasts and personal estimates are for general wellness and informational purposes only.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Why is the UV forecast unavailable?

UV Predict needs an internet connection and a forecast location. You can allow location access while using the app or choose a city manually. Review Location access in iPhone Settings if current-location forecasts do not load. Apple Weather availability can also vary temporarily by region or service status.

### Why is Time in Daylight missing?

With your permission, UV Predict reads Time in Daylight from Apple Health. It does not write Health data. Data availability depends on the supported devices and apps that record this Health category. You can review access in the Health app or iPhone Settings.

### How do I record outdoor time manually?

Pro users can start or stop outdoor time from the Today screen and review today's manual sessions. UV Predict combines overlapping Apple Health and manual intervals only once in the displayed total.

### How do I delete local records?

Open UV Predict, go to Settings, then Data and privacy, and choose Delete local records. This removes manual outdoor records stored by UV Predict. It does not delete records managed by Apple Health. You can also remove individual manual sessions from the daylight records view.

### How do I restore Pro access?

Open UV Predict, go to Settings, and tap Restore Purchases. Use the same Apple Account that made the original purchase.

### How do I manage or cancel the monthly subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple. In UV Predict Settings, active monthly subscribers can tap Manage Subscription. You can also open iPhone Settings, tap your Apple Account name, then tap Subscriptions. The lifetime purchase does not renew.

### Is UV Predict medical advice?

No. UV Predict provides general wellness and planning information. UV forecasts, personal exposure context, skin-response ranges, and Vitamin D opportunity are estimates. They are not medical advice, diagnoses, treatment, direct sensor measurements, or guarantees of safe exposure.
